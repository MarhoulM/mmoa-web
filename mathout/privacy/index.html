Privacy Policy for Mathout

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Mathout helps you practice practical math and number sense. Your exercise progress and app preferences are stored on your device. Optional advertising and the one-time ad-free purchase involve the providers described below.

1. Information stored on your device

Mathout saves your assessment status, Number Score, skill scores and counts, difficulty levels, workout summaries (including completion dates, duration and results), streak information, and a random install seed in local app storage. It also saves your language, theme and answer-mode preferences. Individual answers are used to update this progress; the app's saved history consists of workout summaries rather than a log of every answer.

Mathout also keeps a local copy of the latest known ad-free entitlement state so it can handle startup and temporary purchase-service failures. This copy is not a payment record or proof of ownership. Mathout's own progress and preference code does not upload these records to a Mathout server. Android device backup or transfer settings may separately affect copies of app data.

2. Accounts and direct collection

Mathout does not offer a Mathout account, registration, login or profile. It does not send your exercise progress to a Mathout account backend. A Google Play account used for a purchase, a RevenueCat App User ID, and advertising identifiers are separate from a Mathout account.

Mathout does not send its local exercise progress to an analytics service in its default configuration. Its third-party advertising, consent, purchase and store services may process the data described below.

3. Advertising

Mathout includes a Google AdMob banner placement for Android builds with advertising enabled. Mathout does not use interstitial, rewarded, app-open or native ad placements. A banner request is gated on UMP permitting ad requests and the app determining that ad-free access is not active. No banner is requested while the entitlement state is unknown or ad-free access is confirmed.

The Google Mobile Ads SDK may process information such as IP address, app interactions, diagnostics and device or advertising identifiers to deliver, measure and protect ads, and, where applicable, personalize them according to consent and user settings. See Google's Mobile Ads data disclosure and Google's Privacy Policy.

4. Consent and privacy choices

For an ads-enabled build, Mathout asks Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to update consent information when the app starts and shows a consent form if required. Mathout checks whether UMP permits ad requests before requesting a banner. Where UMP requires a privacy-options entry point, you can reopen those choices from Settings → Privacy options. The Privacy Policy link is available in Settings. The choices and messages shown depend on your region and the messages configured for the app. See Google's UMP information.

5. In-app purchases

Mathout offers an optional one-time “Remove Ads Forever” purchase through Google Play. Google Play handles checkout and payment information. Mathout does not ask for or store your card details. The app reads the resulting purchase and entitlement status to decide whether to show ads, and offers “Restore purchases” in Settings. See Google's Privacy Policy.

6. RevenueCat

When purchase support is configured, Mathout uses RevenueCat to retrieve the ad-free offering, make or restore a purchase, and check the ad_free entitlement. Mathout does not give RevenueCat a custom Mathout account ID. RevenueCat's SDK can create an anonymous App User ID and process that identifier, purchase records, product information and entitlement status to provide this service. This is distinct from your Google Play identity. See RevenueCat's App User ID documentation and RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.

7. Third-party services

  • Google Play and Google Play Billing: app distribution and purchase checkout.
  • Google AdMob / Mobile Ads: banner advertising when enabled.
  • Google UMP: consent messages and privacy choices for ads-enabled builds.
  • RevenueCat: purchase and ad-free entitlement management when configured.

These providers process data under their own privacy practices. Their processing, including any data retained on their systems, is separate from Mathout's local exercise progress.

8. Data retention and your controls

Mathout keeps its local progress, preferences and ad-free status cache on your device until you remove the app's data or uninstall it, subject to Android backup and transfer behavior. The app currently has no in-app progress export or reset feature and no Mathout account to delete. Clearing app data removes local progress and preferences; it may also remove locally cached purchase state, after which “Restore purchases” can ask the purchase services to check ownership again. Removing local data does not itself delete records held by Google or RevenueCat. Their retention and request processes are described in their privacy policies.

You can change Mathout preferences in Settings and, where offered, revisit advertising choices there. You can also manage Android advertising ID controls in your device settings.

9. Children's privacy

Mathout's current app flow does not ask users for their age. Advertising and consent behavior depends on the app's store audience settings and advertising configuration. If you have a question about a child's use of Mathout, contact us using the address below.

10. Security

Mathout's exercise progress is kept in Android app storage. No storage or network service can be guaranteed completely secure. Google and RevenueCat describe their own safeguards in their privacy information linked above.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this page when Mathout or its third-party services change. The date above shows when this version was last updated.

12. Contact

For privacy questions about Mathout, contact mmoa.software@gmail.com.
