4. Information stored on your device (local app data)

Mathout saves assessment status, Number Score, skill scores and counts, difficulty levels, workout summaries (including completion dates, duration and results), streak information, and a random install seed in local app storage. It also saves language, theme and answer-mode preferences. Individual answers update this progress; saved history consists of workout summaries rather than a log of every answer.

Mathout also keeps a local copy of the latest known ad-free entitlement state so it can handle startup and temporary purchase-service failures. This copy is not a payment record or proof of ownership. Mathout’s own progress and preference code does not upload these records to a Mathout server. Android backup or transfer settings may separately copy app data.

5. Accounts and direct collection

Mathout does not offer a Mathout account, registration, login or profile. It does not send exercise progress to a Mathout account backend. A Google Play account used for a purchase, a RevenueCat App User ID, and advertising identifiers (when ads SDKs run) are separate from a Mathout account.

Mathout does not send its local exercise progress to an analytics service in its default configuration. Third-party advertising, consent, purchase and store SDKs may process data as described in their sections when those features are active in the build you use.

6. Advertising (Google AdMob) — active in the current confirmed AAB

Owner-confirmed: the current Android App Bundle distributed by the operator includes Google AdMob banner advertising. Mathout does not use interstitial, rewarded, app-open or native ad placements.

A banner request is still gated in code on UMP permitting ad requests and on the app determining that ad-free access is not active. No banner is requested while entitlement state is unknown or ad-free access is confirmed.

The Mobile Ads SDK may process information such as IP address, app interactions, diagnostics and device or advertising identifiers to deliver, measure and protect ads, and, where applicable, personalize them according to consent and settings. See Google’s Mobile Ads data disclosure and Google’s Privacy Policy. Google and the publisher commonly act as independent controllers for standard AdMob use (see Google’s AdMob GDPR materials); exact role mapping requires legal confirmation.

7. Consent and privacy choices (Google UMP)

Mathout asks Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) to update consent information when the app starts and shows a consent form if required. Mathout checks whether UMP permits ad requests before requesting a banner. Where UMP requires a privacy-options entry point, you can reopen those choices from Settings → Privacy options. The Privacy Policy link is available in Settings. Choices depend on your region and the messages configured for the app. See Google’s UMP information.

Withdrawing consent: where advertising processing relies on consent gathered through UMP, you may withdraw or change that consent via the in-app privacy options (when shown) and via Android advertising / privacy controls. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing based on consent before withdrawal. UMP choices are not automatically treated here as covering every GDPR legal basis for all processing — that requires legal confirmation.

8. Google Play Billing — active in the current confirmed AAB

Owner-confirmed: Mathout uses Google Play Billing for the optional one-time “Remove Ads Forever” purchase. Google Play handles checkout and payment information. Mathout does not ask for or store your card details. The app reads purchase / entitlement status to decide whether ads may be shown, and offers “Restore purchases” in Settings. Purchase-related metadata may be processed by Google under its privacy policy. See Google’s Privacy Policy.

9. RevenueCat — active in the current confirmed AAB

Owner-confirmed: Mathout uses RevenueCat to retrieve the ad-free offering, make or restore a purchase, and check the ad_free entitlement. Mathout does not give RevenueCat a custom Mathout account ID. RevenueCat’s SDK can create an anonymous App User ID and process that identifier, purchase records, product information and entitlement status. This is distinct from your Google Play identity. See RevenueCat’s App User ID documentation and RevenueCat’s Privacy Policy. RevenueCat’s published materials describe processing involving AWS facilities in the United States; transfer safeguards require legal confirmation ([TODO — legal review of DPA / SCCs]).

11. Retention criteria
